# Retro Games Site

### [chiconatello.github.io/NEON_RETRO/](https://chiconatello.github.io/NEON_RETRO/)

Step into the neon-lit arcade of the past. This site is a love letter to classic gaming — a curated collection of the icons that defined entire generations, pulled together from some of the best preservation communities on the web. From dodging ghosts in a maze to blasting through alien invasions, saving princesses, or racing through pixelated streets, every corner of this collection is built for nostalgia. Whether you grew up with a joystick in hand or you're discovering these legends for the first time, there's something here worth playing. Huge thanks to the communities keeping retro gaming history alive — dive in and relive it.
